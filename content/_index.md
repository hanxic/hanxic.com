I am a CS Ph.D. student at Cornell University, advised by [Alexandra Silva](https://alexandrasilva.org/) and [Andrew Myers](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/andru/). My research interests are in programming languages and formal verification, with recent work on probabilistic programming and concurrency. I am also a mentor at Cornell's [Math+AI](https://danielhl.github.io/math-ai.html), working on benchmarks and tools for autoformalization.

Before Cornell, I graduated from the University of Pennsylvania with a B.S.E. and M.S.E. in Computer Science from Penn Engineering and a B.S. in Economics from Wharton. I was a member of Penn's [PLClub](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~plclub/), advised by [Prof. Steve Zdancewic](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~stevez/), where I worked on [Verifying LLVM (Vellvm)](https://github.com/vellvm/vellvm), differential testing for LLVM compilers, and linear logic. I was a [Wharton Research Scholar](https://undergrad-inside.wharton.upenn.edu/wrs/) supervised by [Prof. Sanjeev Khanna](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~sanjeev/) on knapsack problems with precedence constraints.
